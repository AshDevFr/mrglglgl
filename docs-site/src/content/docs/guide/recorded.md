title: What gets recorded
description: Money, chat for role play, Legacy Challenges, and choosing which recorded data the app uses.
---

Besides the day's quests, kills and travels (see [The addon](/guide/addon/)), the addon can record
three more kinds of things. Each shows up in **What happened** on the day's page.

## Money

The addon notes your gold when you log in and out, and what you earn and spend each day, by where
it came from or went: loot, quests, vendors, repairs, trainers, flights, mail, the auction house
and trades. It also records your auction house events: an item listed, sold, won, outbid on,
expired or cancelled.

On a day's page, **What happened** shows **Earned** and **Spent** (each with its sources), **Gold**
(the balance) and **Auction house**. The narrator is told about them too, so a day of selling
everything at the auction house can read like one.

## Chat, for role play

**Chat** is off by default: what people say is not something the addon records unless you ask. If
you role play and want the journal to remember the lines, turn it on in the addon's **Chat** page
(see below).

Each channel has two switches, so you choose whose words are kept:

- **Say: your lines** and **Say: others' lines**, and the same for **Emote**, **Yell**, **Party**,
  **Raid**, **Guild**, **Officer**, **Whisper** and **Numbered channels (General, Trade...)**.

With Chat on, your own say, emote and yell lines are recorded to begin with; everything else stays
off until you turn it on.

Other players' lines are theirs. The app shows them only to you, in **What happened**: they are
never sent to a narrator, and never go into the website or an export. Your own lines are used like
any other recorded data (see **Data used** below).

## Growing up

Beyond levels and quests, the addon notes how your character grows. Each is its own category in
the addon's settings, on by default, and each has its pair of switches under **Data used**:

- **Spells learned**: each spell or ability, druid forms included.
- **Mounts and pets**: each mount and companion pet you add.
- **Reputation**: when a standing rises, such as becoming Friendly with Thunder Bluff. Meeting a
  faction for the first time is not news.
- **Gear upgrades**: an item you put on that beats what that slot held, by item level. Your first
  ring counts; swapping back to a piece you already wore does not.
- **Hearthstone and flight paths**: where you set your hearthstone, and each new flight path.
- **Dungeons and raids**: each one you enter, and how long you stayed.
- **Duels**: duels you win or lose, with your opponent's name. Like other players' chat, their
  names stay in the app: the narrator, the website and exports only hear that you won or lost.

These were added after the game's launch and some of the client's events behind them have not
been seen on WoW: Forever yet. If one is missing, the addon simply records nothing for it.

## Legacy Challenges

On WoW: Forever, Legacy Challenges are achievements worth no points. The addon records each one
you complete, with its name and category, and the app shows them in **What happened** under
**Legacy Challenges** and tells the narrator.

## Choosing which data the app uses

Every kind of recorded data can be kept out of the story. On a character's **⋯** menu, choose
**Data used…**. Each kind (sessions, levels, quests, zones and discoveries, kills and bosses,
deaths, fishing, professions, notable loot, money and the auction house, group members, chat and
Legacy Challenges) has two switches:

- **Narrators, website and exports:** whether it is sent to a narrator, put on the website and
  included in exports.
- **What happened:** whether it is shown in the app.

Turned off, a kind is left out of those places, along with the milestones it earned. Nothing is
deleted: the app keeps everything it has archived, so turning a switch back on brings it back.
This is different from the addon's **Forget** page, which deletes data from the game's files.
