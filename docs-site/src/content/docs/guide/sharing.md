## Copy an entry

**Copy entry** on a day or week copies it to paste into Discord or a forum. The copy ends with
"via Mrglglgl" unless you turn that off in **Settings**, **Writing**.

## Export a journal

**Export** on a character's page saves the whole journal:

- **Web page:** one file laid out like the journal, stamps and captioned screenshots inside, with
  a light and dark switch. Opens in any browser, nothing else needed.
- **PDF:** through the system's print dialog.
- **Markdown:** plain text, for notes apps and wikis.

Only accepted entries are exported, never drafts. A day without an entry exports what happened.
