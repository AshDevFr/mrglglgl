---
title: The journal website
description: Keep a website of your journals up to date, ready to put online.
---

Turn on **Settings**, **Website**, and choose a folder: the app keeps a finished site up to date
in it, with a post per week, an archive, the milestones and a feed. It is plain HTML, ready for
Cloudflare Pages, Netlify or GitHub Pages, with nothing to build.

![The Website tab of Settings.](../../../assets/screenshots/settings-website.png)

- **Characters:** whose journals the site shows is chosen under **Settings**, **Characters**,
  with **On the site**.
- **Stand-in names:** other players appear under invented names unless you turn that off. Realm
  names are left out unless you choose to show them.
- The site only shows finished days and accepted entries: never today, never an entry told so far.

To change how the site looks, from a few colours to every template, see
[Website themes](/reference/website-themes/).
