## Choosing a narrator

**Narrator**, in **Settings**, **Writing**, chooses who writes your journal. The murloc scribe is
the default; the grumpy dwarf, the pompous noble, the smug academic, the easygoing troll and the
silly elf are other styles you can choose. The murloc writing entirely in its own language is only used if you pick it. On any day
or week you can also pick a narrator for that entry alone.

**Murloc margin notes** sets how often the murloc adds a note in the margin, from never to about
one day in three.

**Language of the journal** writes entries in English or French, independently of the app's own
language.

## How long an entry is

A day's entry grows with the day: a short session gets a paragraph or two, about 120 words, and a
long one with much more in it gets up to about 400 words in a few paragraphs. A weekly chapter is
about 250 words. To change one entry's length, rewrite it with a note such as "shorter".

## Writing preferences

**Writing preferences**, in **Settings**, **Writing**, is a note to every narrator about how you
like the prose: "no em dashes", "British spelling", "short sentences". It is sent with every entry
and chapter, and shapes wording only: anything it names is never taken as something that
happened.

## Your own narrators

**Settings**, **Narrators** lists them all.

- **Edit a copy** starts your own narrator from one of the built-in ones.
- **New narrator** starts from scratch, with only the made-up example day and the murloc's part
  filled in for you.
- Your narrators can be edited, exported to a file to share, imported, and deleted.

![The narrators list in Settings.](../../../assets/screenshots/settings-narrators.png)

The editor has a field for each part of a narrator: who they are, whose eyes the day is told
through, how they sound, how the murloc shows, the worked example, and hints for particular kinds
of day. Every built-in narrator writes as the adventurer, saying "I"; your own can instead tell the
day as someone who watched it, calling the adventurer by name. **Preview** writes a sample day
with the narrator before you save it. See [Narrator files](/reference/narrator-files/) for
what each field does and what makes a narrator write well.
