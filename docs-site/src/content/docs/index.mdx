  <Card title="The addon records" icon="pencil">

  </Card>
  <Card title="The app writes" icon="open-book">
    Each finished day and week becomes a short journal entry, written by the narrator you choose,
    checked against what actually happened.
  </Card>
  <Card title="On your computer" icon="laptop">
    Writing runs on a small model on your own computer: no account, no online service. A model
    you run or pay for elsewhere is optional.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Yours to share" icon="rocket">
    Copy an entry for Discord, export the whole journal as a web page, a PDF or Markdown, or keep
    a website of your journals up to date.
  </Card>
